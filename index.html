About the Club

The McCreary Central Drama Club gives Raiders a place to build confidence, creativity, and community through theatre. Members explore acting, improv, stage management, lighting, sound, set design, and everything else it takes to bring a production to life. Whether you dream of the spotlight or prefer working behind the scenes, there's a role for you.

We put on two productions each school year — a fall one-act festival entry and a spring musical or play — along with smaller showcases, improv nights, and community performances throughout the year.
