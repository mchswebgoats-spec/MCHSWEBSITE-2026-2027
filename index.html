About MCHS

Raider Nation is
more than a school.

McCreary Central High School serves students in McCreary County and provides opportunities for students to grow academically, personally, and professionally.

Through academics, career and technical education, athletics, clubs, organizations, and community partnerships, students are encouraged to discover their strengths and prepare for their future.

Our Mission

McCreary Central High School is committed to helping students develop the knowledge, skills, confidence, and character needed to succeed beyond high school.
