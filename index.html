Non-Traditional Instruction (NTI)

2026–2027 School Year

Important information

As we approach cooler weather seasons, it is time to share our 2026–2027 Non-Traditional Instruction (NTI) plan. Our district calendar requires 170 instructional days for students, as mandated at the state level. Kentucky allows districts to use NTI days to support continued instruction instead of traditional snow days.

If an NTI day is used

  1. 1

    Use the link below to access NTI assignments.

  2. 2

    Assignments may be completed on regular paper or in Google Classroom, if the teacher allows.

  3. 3

    All MCHS students must complete NTI assignments for each course in which they are enrolled. This does not include Dual Credit, Virtual, or All Day CADRE students, who should continue using their assigned online learning platforms.

  4. 4

    Students who need help on an NTI day should contact their teacher through ParentSquare or Google Classroom.

  5. 5

    After school returns to its normal schedule, students may arrange to meet with the teacher during Innovation Islands time if they need additional support.

Assignment deadline and grades

Missing NTI work will be entered as 0% in the gradebook. NTI assignments must be submitted no later than one week after returning to regular in-person instruction following NTI day(s).
